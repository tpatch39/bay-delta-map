The Bay has three common sizes

Open tidal water from the Golden Gate to Suisun Bay is about 1,140 km². The nine-county Bay Area used for regional planning is 16 times larger. The USGS San Francisco Bay hydrologic region (HUC 1805) falls in between, and also takes in coastal basins — Tomales–Drake Bays and the San Mateo coast — that drain straight to the Pacific.

“The Delta” usually means the Legal Delta

Water Code §12220 (1959) fixes the Legal Delta at about 2,985 km² (≈738,000 acres). The 2009 Delta Reform Act (§85058) adds the Suisun Marsh for the Delta Plan, so Suisun and Grizzly Bays count as both Bay and Delta depending on who is speaking.

The watershed reaches Oregon and the high Sierra

The Sacramento, San Joaquin and SF Bay regions together drain about 30% of California. Add the Tulare–Buena Vista basin, which mostly drains internally but spills north into the San Joaquin in very wet years, and the figure becomes the often-quoted 40%.
